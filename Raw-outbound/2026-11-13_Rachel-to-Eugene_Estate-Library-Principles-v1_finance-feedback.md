## 4. One addition: lock QuickBooks at each year-end (P4, P8)
**The gap:** QuickBooks is the ledger, but it isn't a document, and past years can still be edited (today: Properties journals dated 30/04/2025; Construction entries back to May 2025).

**Proposal:** at each year-end sign-off, and again when the accounts are filed, register an export of each company's **trial balance and general ledger** as a numbered document. That gives a fixed record of what the accounts were built from. Any later edit then shows up against it instead of disappearing.

## 5. Retention (P7)
- Legal minimum + 2 years, so 8 years for accounts and tax, is fine for finance.
- Loan agreements and guarantees: 8 years **after the loan ends**.
- HMRC time-to-pay and enquiry papers: under legal hold while open.
