# Finance Document Index — v1

**Built** 2026-09-18 by Rachel (AI Finance Assistant). **Scope of this pass:** every finance location on
**Google Drive** (`minda@fishboneconstruction.co.uk`) and **OneDrive/SharePoint**
(`info@fishbonedrylining.onmicrosoft.com`), restricted to **Fishbone Group company** records.
Personal documents are excluded by instruction (Minda, 2026-09-18) and by `CHARTER.md` §4.

## How to read this index

This is a **map, not a second store** (`CHARTER.md` §1). One fact, one home: every entry cites where the
document actually lives. Nothing was copied, moved or modified.

Depth is deliberately mixed, because the estate is large:

| Depth | Applied to | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **File level** | statutory accounts, CT600s, loan agreements/statements, budgets, cash flows, completion statements, SSAS scheme deeds | every document named, with its file id |
| **Series level** | high-volume repeating sets (monthly bank statements, CIS returns, PAYE, per-subcontractor tax folders) | what the series is, its period coverage, count where counted, and where it breaks |
| **Folder level only** | large mixed OneDrive areas not yet ruled company-vs-personal | the folder, its size, and what it appears to hold |

Anything at folder level only is listed in **§6 Not yet indexed** — it is known, not yet opened.
