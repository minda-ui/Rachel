# Adviser papers: summary, meeting brief, minutes, reply

_Adopted by owner decision (Minda), **2026-10-08** ("Adopt all five", skill candidate SC-3). Amendment 49. Built from
Alexey Glukhov's (AGGA) ITC matrix, "archiving" note and FY2026 fee proposal (6–8 Oct 2026). Works inside the
Sandbox Mode rules of `CHARTER.md` and the style of `old-school-finance-writing.md`._

---

## 0. When

A paper or email from an adviser (AGGA, RMT, a solicitor, a lender, an insurer) that bears on the group's finances.
Peter or Victoria route it to Rachel's `Raw/` and the Hub.

## 1. Steps

1. **Capture.** Download the attachment (Composio `GMAIL_GET_ATTACHMENT` on the mailbox that received it) and save it
   to `Raw/Finance`, named `YYYY-MM-DD_<Adviser>_<what>.<ext>`, checksum-verified. Read it in full, formulas as well
   as values for a spreadsheet.
2. **Check it against our own books.** Every figure the adviser relies on is checked against QuickBooks or the
   register before it is repeated (`intercompany-check.md` for balances).
3. **Summary for Minda**, in chat: what it is, the figures in a table, what it leaves out or leaves blank, the points
   to settle, and the decision that is hers. At most three questions; the one that settles the rest first.
4. **Meeting brief** if there is a meeting: one page in `Outputs/`, for Minda only: the proposal in a table, the points
   to settle, what the adviser still owes us, what we have already done, what happens after.
5. **Minutes** from a transcript Minda puts in `Raw/`: what was agreed, actions with owner and date.
6. **Reply draft** in `ops@` (or `minda@`), signed by Rachel, for Minda to send. Nothing leaves without her.
7. **Record**: Hub row, session row, and the register for anything that is itself a record (agreement, engagement
   letter).

## 2. Rules

1. **Minda's private view stays private.** If she says what she intends ("I will accept, but don't tell him"), it goes
   in no draft, no brief the adviser could see, and no Hub row: the shared records say only "Minda's decision".
2. **A date in a reply is a promise** (`old-school-finance-writing.md` §7): ask-by dates are proposals until Minda
   confirms.
3. **Answer what was asked; restate what is still owed.** When the adviser's new email does not answer our last
   questions, the next reply repeats them in one line.
4. **Sources the container cannot reach** (blocked sites): say so, and how the text was obtained.

## 3. Watch-points from the first runs

- A fee proposal can leave a line in scope but **unpriced** (HMRC v QuickBooks comparison, 0 hours): point it out.
- Check who files, and whether a new adviser replaces an old one (RMT): the agent authority with HMRC and Companies
  House has to move.
- Ask for an **engagement letter** before work starts.
